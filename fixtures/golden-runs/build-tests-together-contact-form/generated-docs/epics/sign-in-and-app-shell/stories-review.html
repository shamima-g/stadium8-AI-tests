Sign-in and App Shell

Everyone signs in with email and password, lands on the right page for their role, sees only the menu items their role allows, and gets a clear on-page message if they open a page they can't use.

⠿

Sign in, land on your page, and sign out

You open the app, sign in with your email and password, and land on your role's page (a Visitor on the Contact form, a Support Agent or Admin on the Inbox). If sign-in fails you see a clear message on the sign-in screen, and you can sign out from any page.
New screen · Visitor, Support Agent, Admin
Detail — what "done" means, and what you'll check
Done when:
  • Signing in with a seeded account's correct email and password takes a Visitor to the Contact page and a Support Agent or Admin to the Inbox
  • Submitting a wrong email or password shows an inline "email or password is incorrect" error on the sign-in screen, keeps the entered email, clears the password and moves focus to the error summary
  • When sign-in cannot reach the sign-in service, the sign-in screen shows a connection-problem error that is distinct from the wrong-credentials error
  • While signed out, any app URL typed into the address bar, including the app root, sends the user to the sign-in screen instead of the welcome page or the requested page
  • After signing out, pressing the browser Back button returns the user to the sign-in screen rather than revealing the previous app page
  • The sign-in screen has no automated accessibility violations, every field is labelled, and the whole form can be completed with the keyboard alone
You'll check:
  • Open the app while signed out → you land on the sign-in page, not a welcome page
  • Sign in as visitor@example.com / Test123 → you land on the Contact page
  • Sign in as agent@example.com (or admin@example.com) / Test123 → you land on the Inbox
  • Sign in with the wrong password → you see a message saying the email or password is incorrect, and your email is still filled in
  • Type the password → it is shown as dots, never as plain text
  • Sign in, click Sign out, then press the browser Back button → you're sent to sign-in, not back into the app
  • Using only the Tab and Enter keys, sign in successfully

Role-based menu and permission-denied banner

Once signed in, the top menu shows only what your role can use (a Visitor sees Contact and My messages; a Support Agent or Admin sees Inbox). If you open a page your role can't use from a direct link, the page shows a banner explaining which permission you're missing, with a link back to your own page.
Updates the app frame · Visitor, Support Agent, Admin
Detail — what "done" means, and what you'll check
Done when:
  • A signed-in Visitor sees exactly "Contact" and "My messages" in the top menu, with no Inbox item shown in any form
  • A signed-in Support Agent or Admin sees exactly "Inbox" in the top menu, with no Contact or My messages item shown in any form
  • A Visitor who opens the Inbox address directly sees a permission-denied banner on the page naming the missing permission, and its link takes them back to the Contact page
  • A Support Agent or Admin who opens the Contact or My messages address directly sees the permission-denied banner, and its link takes them back to the Inbox
  • The permission-denied banner is announced to screen readers when the page loads and its back link can be reached and followed with the keyboard
You'll check:
  • Sign in as the Visitor → the top menu shows only Contact and My messages
  • Sign in as the Support Agent, then the Admin → the top menu shows only Inbox
  • As the Visitor, type /inbox in the address bar → you see a "you don't have permission" banner on the page (no error page) that names the missing permission
  • Click the banner's link → you're taken back to the Contact page
  • As the Support Agent, type /contact in the address bar → you see the banner, and its link takes you to the Inbox
  • Using only the Tab key, reach the banner's link and press Enter → it takes you to your page

Session timeouts and account lockout

Your session ends after 30 minutes without activity, or after 12 hours in total, and you're taken back to the sign-in screen with a message that your session expired. After 5 wrong passwords in a row the account is locked for 15 minutes, and the sign-in screen tells you so.
Updates the sign-in screen · Visitor, Support Agent, Admin
Detail — what "done" means, and what you'll check
Done when:
  • On the 5th consecutive failed sign-in for an account, the sign-in screen says the account is locked and for how long (15 minutes)
  • While an account is locked, signing in with its correct password is refused and the locked message is shown again
  • Once 15 minutes have passed since the lockout, the account can sign in with the correct password and lands on its role's page
  • After 30 minutes with no activity, a signed-in user is returned to the sign-in screen with a message that their session expired
  • A user who keeps working is still signed out with the session-expired message once 12 hours have passed since signing in
You'll check:
  • Enter the wrong password for agent@example.com five times → the sign-in screen says the account is locked for 15 minutes
  • While it's locked, enter the correct password → you still can't sign in and see the locked message
  • Sign in as another seeded user while that account is locked → that user signs in normally

Enquiry and user records with sample data

Under the hood — nothing to click; the next step proves it works.
The app holds enquiries and users in memory, starting with sample enquiries that cover every category and every status, so the later Contact, My messages and Inbox screens have data to show. Changes last until the page is reloaded.
