## Project Overview

**Contact & Enquiry Management** — A public-facing contact form for Visitors to submit enquiries (name, email, optional address, category, and comment), with a role-gated back-office inbox where Support Agents triage enquiries through a status lifecycle (New -> In Progress -> Resolved) and Admins can additionally delete spam or duplicates.

- **Roles:** Visitor (submits enquiries, views own), Support Agent (triages inbox), Admin (can also delete)
- **Auth:** Server-side (simulated client-side for prototype)
- **Data:** Mock-only with in-memory fixtures
